# Selected Direction: Foundry Black

## Design Movement

**Contemporary editorial art direction** informed by Swiss typographic systems, independent design studios, and the dark-to-light spatial drama of the supplied Zuko reference. The work will be an original interpretation, not a brand or content clone.

## Core Principles

1. **Typography is architecture.** Oversized display text establishes rhythm and provides visual structure before decoration.
2. **Contrast carries hierarchy.** Near-black, paper-white, and restrained clay-red create emphasis without gradients or visual noise.
3. **Editorial asymmetry over dashboard grids.** Sections shift their alignments, allowing images and text to take turns as the focal point.
4. **Motion reveals intent.** Hover and scroll motion is purposeful: it exposes a project preview, sharpens a focal state, or advances the narrative.

## Color Philosophy

The interface begins in **coal black** to create gallery-like focus and transitions to **paper white** for reading comfort. A single **burnt clay** accent functions like an editorial correction mark: sparingly placed on availability, project indices, and active states to signal craft and confidence.

## Layout Paradigm

The page is a **vertical editorial sequence** rather than a centered marketing funnel. The hero is cropped like a magazine cover; the statement section occupies an offset text column; project cases alternate between broad visual spans and narrow annotation rails. Section dividers are thin rules and generous negative space, not rounded cards.

## Signature Elements

1. **The Split Monogram:** a two-piece geometric mark, repeated in the header, favicon, and motion cues.
2. **Numbered Work Rail:** a vertical annotation system pairing project indices with category tags and a directional arrow.
3. **Clay Locator Dots:** small burnt-clay markers that identify availability, active navigation, and selected projects.

## Interaction Philosophy

Every interactive element provides a clear editorial response: project images slightly reframe and reveal an arrow on hover; text links underline from the left; the menu collapses into a simple overlay on mobile. Buttons remain square-edged, direct, and tactile rather than pill-shaped.

## Animation

On load, hero copy rises a few pixels while its opacity resolves in a staggered sequence. Scroll reveals fade sections upward by a restrained distance. Work imagery uses subtle scaling and image-position shifts on hover. Durations remain between 160 ms and 480 ms with a firm cubic-bezier ease-out. Motion is disabled for users who prefer reduced motion.

## Typography System

**DM Sans** is the display face, deployed at extreme weights and tight tracking for headlines and project titles. **IBM Plex Mono** is used for metadata, labels, dates, and navigation utilities. Headlines use sentence case, whilst labels use small uppercase letterspacing. The type scale is fluid and avoids generic, uniform paragraph hierarchy.

## Brand Essence

**A portfolio for a multidisciplinary digital designer who turns abstract ideas into clear, emotionally resonant digital work.**

Personality: **assured, tactile, incisive.**

## Brand Voice

Headlines are confident and plainly specific; CTAs invite a purposeful next step; microcopy reads like project annotation rather than sales copy.

Example lines:

> I turn unfinished ideas into digital experiences with a point of view.

> View the thinking behind the pixels.

## Wordmark & Logo

The primary mark is a **broken square with an offset clay dot**: it suggests a frame, a cursor, and a designer’s point of intervention. The name appears separately as a tight, custom-styled wordmark rather than in a default font treatment.

## Signature Brand Color

**Foundry Clay — #A6503D**

## Style Decisions

- Preserve the supplied reference’s premium, image-led, dark-to-light editorial rhythm without copying its name, visuals, copy, or trademarked identity.
- Use a deep charcoal hero with light text over imagery, and paper-white reading sections with charcoal text.
- Avoid generic centered hero blocks, purple gradients, pill-heavy components, and Inter typography.
